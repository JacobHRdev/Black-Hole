What am I seeing?
The dark center is the event horizon: beyond it, no signal can return. The glowing disk is matter spiraling inward. The orange ring marks the ISCO, while the pale ring marks the photon sphere. This is an educational Newtonian approximation using the Paczyński–Wiita potential, not a full general-relativity ray tracer.
